\section{Assessment Methodology}
% ================================================================

This self-assessment follows the methodology established by the Basel Committee
in its progress reports on adoption of BCBS~239 (2013, 2014). Each of the 14
principles is scored on a four-point scale:

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{cl}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Score} & \textbf{Rating} \\
        \midrule
        4 & \textcolor{green!60!black}{Fully Compliant} --- All requirements met \\
        3 & \textcolor{blue}{Largely Compliant} --- Most requirements met, minor gaps \\
        2 & \textcolor{orange}{Materially Non-compliant} --- Significant gaps remain \\
        1 & \textcolor{red}{Non-compliant} --- Requirements not addressed \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The 14 principles are organised into four categories as defined by BCBS~239:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Governance and Infrastructure} (Principles 1--2): Overarching
          governance arrangements and data architecture.
    \item \textbf{Risk Data Aggregation} (Principles 3--6): Capabilities for
          accurate, complete, timely, and adaptable data aggregation.
    \item \textbf{Risk Reporting Practices} (Principles 7--11): Quality,
          comprehensiveness, clarity, frequency, and distribution of reports.
    \item \textbf{Supervisory Review} (Principles 12--14): Supervisory
          expectations for review, remediation, and cross-border cooperation.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Principle Description}
A bank's risk data aggregation capabilities and risk reporting practices should be subject to strong governance arrangements consistent with other principles and guidance established by the Committee.

\paragraph{Evidence of Compliance}
Model Risk Committee (MRC) established with formal Terms of Reference. Quarterly meeting cycle adopted. Tiering matrix (Materiality x Complexity) applied per Handbook Chapter 8. RAG ratings assigned to all models. Audit trail tracks all governance decisions with timestamps.

\paragraph{Identified Gaps}
Data governance policy not yet formalised as a standalone document. Board-level reporting on risk data quality not yet established.

\paragraph{Remediation Plan}
Draft standalone data governance policy. Establish quarterly board reporting on risk data quality metrics. (Target: 2026-06-30)

\subsection{Principle 2: Data Architecture and IT Infrastructure}

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{ll}
        \toprule
        Score & \textcolor{blue}{\textbf{3/4 --- Largely Compliant}} \\
        Target Date & 2026-06-30 \\
        \bottomrule

\paragraph{Principle Description}
A bank should design, build and maintain data architecture and IT infrastructure which fully supports its risk data aggregation capabilities and risk reporting practices not only in normal times but also during times of stress or crisis.

\paragraph{Evidence of Compliance}
Centralised model architecture in src/models/ with clear separation of concerns. JSON data store with structured schemas (CDM layer). Model chain documented with field-level data handoffs between all 8 models. Configurable catchment-based data organisation.

\paragraph{Identified Gaps}
No formal data dictionary or metadata catalogue. Single-node architecture without high-availability or disaster recovery provisions.

\paragraph{Remediation Plan}
Create formal data dictionary covering all model inputs/outputs. Document data flow diagrams. Assess HA/DR requirements. (Target: 2026-06-30)

\subsection{Principle 3: Accuracy and Integrity}

\begin{table}[H]

\paragraph{Principle Description}
A bank should be able to generate accurate and reliable risk data to meet normal and stress/crisis reporting accuracy requirements. Data should be aggregated on a largely automated basis so as to minimise the probability of errors.

\paragraph{Evidence of Compliance}
518 automated tests (464 Python + 51 JavaScript) all passing. GEV statistical fitting with validated return periods. Depth-damage curves calibrated against EA data. Monte Carlo simulations with VaR/ES calculations. Automated parameter inventory tracking \textasciitilde{}150+ parameters with source references.

\paragraph{Identified Gaps}
No formal data reconciliation process between model outputs. Limited stress testing of data aggregation under extreme scenarios.

\paragraph{Remediation Plan}
Implement automated reconciliation checks between model chain outputs. Design stress test scenarios for data aggregation pipeline. (Target: 2026-09-30)

\subsection{Principle 4: Completeness}

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{ll}
        \toprule
        Score & \textcolor{blue}{\textbf{3/4 --- Largely Compliant}} \\
        Target Date & 2026-09-30 \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}

\paragraph{Principle Description}
A bank should be able to capture and aggregate all material risk data across the bank. Data should be available by business line, legal entity, asset type, industry, region and other groupings that permit identifying and reporting risk exposures, concentrations and emerging risks.

\paragraph{Evidence of Compliance}
8 models covering the complete risk chain: Storm Intensity, Storm-Gauge Response, GEV Hazard Curves, PRS Pricing, Depth-Damage, Property Valuation, Mortgage Pricer, Risk Assessor. Data aggregated by catchment, gauge, property, and mortgage portfolio dimensions. Property hazard curves link spatial flood risk to individual assets.

\paragraph{Identified Gaps}
Limited to physical flood risk; does not yet cover other risk types (market, credit, operational). Geographic coverage limited to configured catchments.

\paragraph{Remediation Plan}
Document scope limitations clearly. Assess extensibility to additional risk types and geographic regions. (Target: 2026-09-30)

\paragraph{Principle Description}
Risk management reports should accurately and precisely convey aggregated risk data and reflect risk in an exact manner. Reports should be reconciled and validated.

\paragraph{Evidence of Compliance}
Automated PDF generation for meeting packs, parameter inventories, test results, and model documentation. ReportLab-based report generation with structured tables and formatted output. LaTeX document generation for formal governance documents (ToR, parameter inventory, test reports).

\paragraph{Identified Gaps}
No formal report validation or sign-off process. No automated reconciliation between dashboard displays and PDF reports.

\paragraph{Remediation Plan}
Implement report validation checksums. Establish formal report sign-off workflow in MRC process. (Target: 2026-06-30)

\paragraph{Principle Description}
Risk management reports should cover all material risk areas within the organisation. The depth and scope of these reports should be consistent with the size and complexity of the bank's operations and risk profile.

\paragraph{Principle Description}
Supervisors should periodically review and evaluate a bank's compliance with the eleven Principles.

\paragraph{Evidence of Compliance}
MRC quarterly review cycle with documented agenda, minutes, decisions, and actions. Audit trail captures all governance changes with timestamps. RAG ratings reviewed at each MRC meeting. Model validation status tracked in inventory.

\paragraph{Remediation Plan}

Supervisors should have and use the appropriate tools and resources to require effective and timely remedial action by a bank to address deficiencies in its risk data aggregation capabilities and risk reporting practices.
